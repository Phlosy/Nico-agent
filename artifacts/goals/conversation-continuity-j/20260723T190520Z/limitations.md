- `semantic-completion-v1` is a focused semantic floor, not the complete
  parent RH-H obligation gate. Tool, approval, Artifact, budget and other
  database obligations remain open.
- The Gate evaluates structured model intent/completion claims plus frozen
  Runtime facts. U11 must measure model calibration and wrong-intent behavior.
- Strict final envelope enforcement is scoped to Native Direct/ReAct/Plan.
  Mock and Hermes compatibility behavior is unchanged.
- No credentialed external provider was configured. Hermetic protocol and
  persistence behavior are verified; external model quality is unverified.
