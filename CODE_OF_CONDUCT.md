# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Nico Agent community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Until a dedicated conduct address is published, use the repository's [private GitHub reporting channel](https://github.com/Phlosy/Nico-agent/security/advisories/new) and identify the report as a Code of Conduct matter. Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in a public Issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the privacy and safety of reporters where possible, and may remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according to severity and context.
